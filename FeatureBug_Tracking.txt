  Known limitations (not bugs, no fix planned):
  - Passwords are passed to 7z as -p<pw> on the command line, so they are visible in
    /proc/<pid>/cmdline to other local users. 7-Zip offers no password-file or
    stdin-password option; there is no fix short of a wrapper.
  - Browsing a .tar.gz/.tar.bz2/.tar.xz shows the inner .tar as a single entry, because
    that is what 7z lists. Creating and extracting them work correctly (2.3.0); making
    the tree descend into the inner tar would need transparent repacking for
    delete/rename and is a much larger change.
